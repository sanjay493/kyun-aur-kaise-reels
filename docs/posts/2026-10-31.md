## v003 Why do you see lightning before you hear thunder?
Series: Science Around Us · CBSE Class 8–9 (Sound; Some Natural Phenomena) · 24.7 s · 64 words

Voiceover:
Lightning and thunder happen together. So why do you see the flash first? Light is super fast, about three lakh kilometres every second. Sound is much slower, about three hundred forty metres a second. The flash reaches you instantly. The rumble is still on its way. Count the seconds between flash and thunder, then divide by three. Nine seconds? The storm is about three kilometres away.

Caption:
Your monsoon storm has a built-in distance meter ⚡ Flash → count → ÷ 3 = kilometres.
Bonus safety rule: if you can hear thunder, you're close enough to be struck. Go indoors and wait about 30 minutes after the last rumble.

## v004 Do you need your UPI PIN to receive money?
Series: Smart & Safe · 23.6 s · 69 words

Voiceover:
Someone wants to buy your old cycle online. They send a QR code to pay you. "Scan it and enter your UPI PIN to receive the money," they say. Stop. That's a trap. Your UPI PIN is needed only when money leaves your account. Enter it there, and the money goes from you to them. To receive money, you never scan or enter a PIN. Remember: PIN means paying.

Caption:
Selling old books, a cycle or a phone online? 🚲 A real buyer just pays you. If anyone asks you to scan a QR code or type your UPI PIN "to receive", it's a scam.
Lost money? Call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
Send this to a parent who sells things online 📲

## v005 Is getting angry always bad?
Series: Teen Talk · 24.5 s · 67 words

Voiceover:
Your brother grabs your phone without asking, and your face goes hot. Is that anger bad? Not really. Psychologists call anger a normal, usually healthy emotion. It's a signal that something feels unfair. But shouting or slamming doors usually makes things worse. Try this: pause, take one slow breath, and name it. "I feel angry because..." Anger is okay. What you do with it is your choice.

Caption:
Feeling angry doesn't make you a bad person 💗 It's information. Pause, breathe, and put it into words before you act.
Parents: naming the feeling together ("You seem angry because…") helps teens do it themselves.

## v006 Why do we say "an hour" but "a university"?
Series: English Made Easy (new) · CBSE Class 6 English grammar (articles) · 22.7 s · 66 words

Voiceover:
Why do we say 'an hour', but 'a university'? Here's the secret. 'A' and 'an' follow the first sound, not the first letter. 'Hour' has a silent H. It starts with a vowel sound, so: an hour. 'University' starts with a 'you' sound. That's a consonant sound, so: a university. That's why it's an MLA, but a one-rupee coin. Don't trust the letter. Trust your ears.

Caption:
The a/an rule your grammar book hinted at but didn't spell out 👂 Say the word aloud first, then pick the article.
Quick test: an honest man · a European trip · an MBA · a unicorn
