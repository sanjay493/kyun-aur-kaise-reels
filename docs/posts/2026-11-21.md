## v055 My friend and I, or my friend and me?
Series: English Made Easy · all classes · 21.3 s · 69 words

Voiceover:
Me and my friend went out. Or is it my friend and I? Take your friend out. Me went? No. I went. So, my friend and I went. Now: Mummy called my friend and I. Remove the friend. Mummy called I? No. Mummy called me. So, Mummy called my friend and me. And to be polite, name the other person first. Remove the friend. What sounds right stays right.

Caption:
"I" is the one doing the action (the subject). "Me" is the one the action happens to, or comes after words like to, with, for and between (the object) ✍️
That's why it's "between you and me", not "between you and I".
"Me and my friend" is common in casual chat, but in writing and exams use "My friend and I went".
Try these: ___ and Riya made the poster. Ma'am gave Riya and ___ a star. Come with Riya and ___. (Answers: Riya and I, me, me)

## v056 A wedding card that ends in .apk?
Series: Smart & Safe · all classes, parents, grandparents · 26.0 s · 70 words

Voiceover:
WhatsApp: You're invited, open the wedding card. But the file ends in dot A.P.K. That's an app, not a card. Install it, and scammers can control your phone. They can read your O.T.P.s and get into your bank account. Some even send the same fake invite to your contacts. Real cards come as photos or P.D.F.s. Not sure? Call the sender. Dot A.P.K. is not an invitation. Don't install it.

Caption:
Got a wedding invite on WhatsApp? Check the file name first. Police in Rajasthan, Himachal Pradesh and Maharashtra have warned about fake invites sent as files like "marriage.apk" 💌
An .apk is an Android app installer. Real invites come as images (.jpg) or PDFs, never as apps.
The message may even come from a relative's number if their phone was hacked, so check with them by calling.
Lost money or installed one by mistake? Call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in, and tell your bank straight away.
Share this with the family WhatsApp group.

## v057 Why is AMBULANCE written backwards?
Series: Science Around Us · Class 7 (Light: images in a plane mirror) · 23.8 s · 68 words

Voiceover:
Why is the word ambulance written backwards on the front of an ambulance? Hold a word up to a mirror. Left becomes right, and right becomes left. But it's not upside down. Only the sides swap. The driver ahead sees the ambulance in the rear-view mirror. The mirror flips the letters back, so the driver reads ambulance and gives way. Written backwards, so a mirror reads it forwards.

Caption:
A plane mirror's image is upright, the same size as the object, and as far behind the mirror as the object is in front of it 🪞
Try it: write your name on paper and hold it up to a mirror. Now raise your right hand in front of the mirror. Which hand does your image raise?
Letters like A, H, M, O, T, U, V, W, X and Y look the same in a mirror. Can you make a word using only those?
#ScienceAroundUs #CBSEScience #Class7Science #Light #EverydayScience

Sources: NCERT Class 7 Science, Chapter 11 "Light" (Boojho sees "the word 'AMBULANCE' in front was written in a strange manner"; "When the driver of a vehicle ahead of an ambulance looks in her/his rear view mirror, she/he can read 'AMBULANCE' written on it and give way to it"; "in the mirror the 'right' appears 'left' and the 'left' appears 'right'"; "only sides are interchanged; the image does not appear upside down"; image "is erect, of the same size and is at the same distance from the mirror") via IIT Kanpur SATHEE https://sathee.iitk.ac.in/ncert-books/class-07/science/chapter-11-light

## v058 Is the big pack always cheaper?
Series: Money Smart · Class 6–8 (unit rate, division) · 26.3 s · 68 words

Voiceover:
Biscuits: two hundred grams for forty rupees, or five hundred grams for one hundred ten. Big pack's cheaper, right? Compare the same amount. Small pack: twenty rupees per hundred grams. Big pack: twenty two rupees per hundred grams. It costs more! Since twenty twenty two, packs in India must show a unit sale price, to make comparing easier. Bigger isn't always cheaper. Compare the same amount. Then decide.

Caption:
The prices in this video are an example, but always check: a bigger pack is not automatically the cheaper buy 🛒
The trick: divide the price by the quantity so both packs are compared for the same amount (per 100 g, per kg or per litre).
Look for the "unit sale price" on the pack label: it does this division for you.
Even when the big pack is cheaper per gram, it's only a saving if you'll actually finish it.
Try it: 1 litre of oil for ₹150 or 5 litres for ₹780. Which is cheaper per litre? (Answer: 1 litre costs ₹150; 5 litres works out to ₹156 a litre)
#MoneySmart #FinancialLiteracy #SmartShopping #ConsumerAwareness #CBSEMaths

Sources: Press Information Bureau, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, "Centre amends the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011 for enhanced protection of Consumer Rights" (8 Nov 2021; "A new provision has been introduced to indicate the unit sale price on pre packed commodities which will allow easier comparison of the prices of the commodities at the time of purchase"; "The amendments to come into effect from 1st April, 2022") https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1770064 ;
Arithmetic checked: 40 ÷ 2 = 20; 110 ÷ 5 = 22; 780 ÷ 5 = 156.
