## v043 Can ₹7,371 be split among 9 friends?
Series: Maths Without Fear · Class 8 (Number Play, divisibility by 9) · 25.8 s · 67 words

Voiceover:
Can seven thousand three hundred seventy one rupees be split equally among nine friends? Add the digits. Seven, three, seven, one. That's eighteen, and eighteen divides by nine. Why? Ten is nine plus one. A hundred is ninety nine plus one. The nines always divide. Only the digits are left over. So yes: eight hundred nineteen rupees each. Add the digits. The nines take care of themselves.

Caption:
The full working: 7,371 = 7 × 1000 + 3 × 100 + 7 × 10 + 1 = (7 × 999 + 3 × 99 + 7 × 9) + (7 + 3 + 7 + 1). The first bracket always divides by 9, so only the digit sum decides 🧮
Same trick works for 3, because 9, 99 and 999 all divide by 3 as well.
Try these: is 4,536 divisible by 9? What about 8,102? (Answers: yes, 4+5+3+6 = 18; no, 8+1+0+2 = 11)
#MathsWithoutFear #CBSEMaths #Class8Maths #MathsTricks #NCERT

Sources: NCERT Ganita Prakash Class 8, Chapter 5 "Number Play", "Shortcut for Divisibility by 9" ("10 = 9 + 1 ... 100 = 99 + 1, 1000 = 999 + 1"; "If a number is divisible by 9, then the sum of its digits is divisible by 9") via Teachoo https://www.teachoo.com/26936/5680/Shortcut-for-Divisibility-by-9/category/Shortcut-for-Divisibility-by-9/ ;
Allen, NCERT Solutions Class 8 Maths Chapter 5 Number Play, Ex 5.2 ("A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 9"; "abc = 100a + 10b + c = 99a + 9b + (a + b + c)"; same rule for 3) https://allen.in/ncert-solutions/class-8-maths-chapter-5-number-play-ex-5-2 ;
Arithmetic checked: 9 × 819 = 7,371; 4,536 ÷ 9 = 504.

## v044 Why does a steel katori float?
Series: Science Around Us · Class 9 (Gravitation: buoyancy, Archimedes' principle) · 23.5 s · 68 words

Voiceover:
Drop a steel spoon in a bucket, and it sinks. But a steel katori floats. Why? Same steel. Giant ships are made of steel too. Water pushes up on everything in it. That push is called upthrust. Archimedes found it equals the weight of the water pushed aside. A hollow katori or ship pushes aside lots of water, enough to hold up its weight. Same steel. Shape decides.

Caption:
Your Class 9 book says it in one line: objects with density less than the liquid float, objects with density greater than the liquid sink. A hollow katori or ship counts its air too, so its average density is less than water's 🚢
Try it: place an empty steel katori gently on water in a bucket. Now slowly pour water into the katori. When does it sink, and why?
Archimedes' principle is also used to design ships and submarines.

## v045 Your page is all yellow. Did you learn it?
Series: Study Smart · all classes · 24.2 s · 63 words

Voiceover:
Your whole page is yellow with highlighter. But did you actually learn it? Researchers reviewed ten popular study techniques. Highlighting was rated low. So was rereading. It feels busy, but students got little benefit from it. The top two? Testing yourself, and spreading study over several days. So after highlighting, close the book and explain each point from memory. Colour less. Recall more.

Caption:
The 2013 review, led by psychologist John Dunlosky at Kent State University, checked the evidence for ten common study techniques. Highlighting, underlining, rereading and summarising were among those rated low utility; practice testing and spreading study out got the top rating ✍️
A simple upgrade: highlight only one line per paragraph, then turn each highlight into a question in the margin. Next day, cover the page and answer them.

## v046 Your brain is still under construction
Series: Teen Talk · all classes, parents · 24.7 s · 70 words

Voiceover:
Surprise: your brain keeps developing till your mid to late twenties. The prefrontal cortex, behind your forehead, is one of the last to mature. It helps you plan and decide. So teens may respond to stress differently from adults. But a growing brain is ready to learn. Tough subjects, sport and music strengthen it. Stressed? Notice what set it off, then try what calms you. Under construction. Build it well.

Caption:
Adolescence is a big time for brain development, and most teens go on to become healthy adults. Challenging classes, exercise and creative activities like art or music can help the brain mature 🧠
Sleep matters too: in teens the sleep hormone melatonin stays high later at night and drops later in the morning. Good sleep helps mental health too.
If stress or low mood keeps getting in the way of daily life, talk to a parent, teacher, school counsellor or doctor. You don't have to handle it alone.
