## v027 Does the Moon really change shape?
Series: Science Around Us · Class 6–8 (Moon, phases) · 23.4 s · 70 words

Voiceover:
Round one night, a thin smile a week later. Does the Moon really change shape? No. The Moon shines by reflecting sunlight, and half of it is always lit. As it circles the Earth, we see different amounts of that lit half. Lit side facing us? Full moon. Facing away? New moon. One full cycle takes about twenty-nine and a half days. The shape never changes. Only our view does.

Caption:
A common mix-up: phases are NOT Earth's shadow on the Moon 🌙 Earth's shadow only falls on the Moon during a lunar eclipse, which happens at full moon.
Why it matters for festivals: many dates in the Indian calendar follow the Moon. Purnima is the full moon and Amavasya is the new moon, about 29.5 days apart from one Purnima to the next.
Try it: tonight, note the Moon's shape and which side is bright. Check again in 3 or 4 days.

## v028 Shared your WhatsApp code by mistake?
Series: Smart & Safe · all classes, parents · 25.4 s · 70 words

Voiceover:
Someone tricks you into sharing your WhatsApp code. Is your account gone? It could be. With only that code, they can move your account to their phone. Add a second lock: two-step verification, a six-digit PIN only you know. Then a new phone needs your PIN too, not just the S.M.S. code. Turn it on in Settings, Account, Two-step verification. Codes can be stolen. Add a lock only you know.

Caption:
A common trick: a "friend" messages "I sent you a code by mistake, please forward it" 🔐 That code is the key to YOUR account. Never forward it.
Two tips when you set the PIN: don't use your birthday or 123456, and add your email so you can reset the PIN if you forget it. WhatsApp will ask for the PIN every so often, so keep it memorable.
Parents: takes one minute. Do it on your phone too.

## v029 Why is a triangle's area HALF?
Series: Maths Without Fear · Class 7 (area of a triangle) · 24.6 s · 70 words

Voiceover:
A triangle's area is half of base times height. Why half? Draw a rectangle around it, same base, same height. A line from the top corner straight down splits it into two rectangles. Each one is cut in half by a side of the triangle. So the triangle is exactly half the rectangle. Base eight, height five? That makes forty, and half is twenty. Every triangle is half a rectangle.

Caption:
Remember: the height must be at right angles to the base, not the slanted side 📐
What if the top corner sits outside the base (an obtuse triangle)? Two copies of the triangle fit together to make a parallelogram with the same base and height. A parallelogram's area is base × height, so the triangle is still half.
Try it: a triangle has base 12 cm and height 7 cm. What is its area? Answer in the comments.

## v030 Is saying no to a friend mean?
Series: Teen Talk · all classes, parents · 22.2 s · 69 words

Voiceover:
Your friend wants to copy your homework, again. Is saying no mean? No. Saying no to a request isn't saying no to the friend. Try a kind, clear no. I can't share my answers, but I can explain the sum. Keep it short. You don't owe a long excuse. If they push, repeat it calmly. Same words, same kindness. You can say no and still be a good friend.

Caption:
A kind no has two parts: the no, and what you CAN do 🤝 "I can't lend my notes tonight, but I'll send you the page numbers." "I can't come today, but let's go on Sunday."
It's okay to feel a bit awkward afterwards. That feeling passes.
Parents and teachers: helping a child practise one or two of these lines at home makes them easier to say at school.
